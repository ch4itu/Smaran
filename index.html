SMARAN

One username and password.
No wallet. No seed phrase. No crypto to buy or acquire.

Powered by MagicBlock

I wanted to try a blockchain game. It asked me to connect a wallet, and I had to buy crypto before I could even find out whether I liked the game. Trying an app should be as easy as picking a username and password. That’s why I built Smaran. You can try a game first and bring the same account to the next app you choose.

One account works across any Solana apps that add Smaran. Play cricket on your laptop, see the relic you won in another game on your phone, or use the same login to keep a daily habit.

Who pays? You pay 0 SOL to sign in, play or save in the featured Devnet apps. The Smaran Save Fund helps cover the cost of saving to Solana. Each app account pays for its saves, and anyone can use the fund to refill an eligible account when it runs low.

Pick an app

  • Magic OverCricket duels against the computer or a friend. One four or six earns a relic.
  • EternalBlissAn adventure game with quests, battles and shared worlds.
  • SmaranWorldA shared world to walk and chat in: 144 villages across fields, forests, hills, desert and tundra. Relic holders keep them.
  • Ludo FreeflowLudo with no turns: everyone moves at once. Online, a match starts 30 seconds after the second player joins, with 2 to 4 players. Fair dice and a leaderboard.
  • StreaksNot a game: keep a daily habit, one check-in a day.

Our philosophy
You should be able to try an app before spending anything and keep using your account if its original website closes. Smaran’s sign-in page is open source. Once your encrypted signing key is saved to Solana, another host can serve the page and you can unlock the same account with your password. How sign-in works.
